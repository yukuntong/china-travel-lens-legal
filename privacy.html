This Privacy Policy explains how China Travel Lens (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

China Travel Lens is a travel utility for visitors in China. It helps with translation, menus, kiosk screens, landmarks, city guides, and related travel assistance. The App is provided by an individual developer.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Device identifier. A device-based ID used to manage credits, purchase restoration, and anti-abuse controls. The App does not require a traditional username/password account.
  • Photos and images. When you use camera or photo features (for example menu translation, signs, kiosk guidance, or landmark recognition), images you choose or capture may be processed to provide the requested result.
  • Audio. When you use voice translation or speech input, microphone audio may be processed for recognition and translation. Recording starts only after you take an action (such as tapping the mic).
  • Location. Approximate or precise location may be used when you enable location-related features (for example suggesting a city or assisting metro guidance). Location is not collected continuously in the background for advertising.
  • Purchase and credit data. Product IDs, transaction identifiers, receipts/tokens, credit balance, and related records needed to deliver and verify in-app purchases.
  • Technical logs. Limited diagnostic information such as error codes, feature names, timestamps, and request identifiers to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Text you submit. Text you type or paste for translation or guidance features.

3. How we use information

  • Provide translation, OCR, guidance, and travel features you request
  • Process and verify in-app purchases and credit balances
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud/abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Comply with applicable laws and store review requirements

We do not sell your personal information.

4. On-device vs. online processing

Some features can run on your device (for example certain offline translation paths). Other features send content you submit (such as text, photos, or audio) to our cloud services for AI processing so we can return results. Online features may consume credits.

5. Sharing with service providers

We may share information with processors only as needed to operate the App, including:

  • Cloud hosting / API providers that power AI and credit services
  • App store billing platforms (for example Huawei AppGallery IAP, and where applicable Google Play / Apple) for purchase verification
  • On-device SDKs such as speech/OCR/translation libraries used inside the App

These providers process data under their own terms and only for the purposes described above.

6. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain credit records, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Credit entitlements may expire according to the in-app package rules. Transient request content used for a single AI task is processed to fulfill that request and is not kept longer than needed for operations and abuse prevention.

7. Your choices

  • You can deny camera, microphone, or location permission in system settings.
  • You can stop using a feature that requires those permissions.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by emailing us.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience travel tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. International users

If you use the App while traveling, your information may be processed in regions where our servers or service providers operate. We take steps appropriate to the nature of the data and applicable requirements.

11. Changes
